\begin{abstract}
Between keeping every shot as a noisy training example and averaging all shots
into one clean feature lies an entire spectrum of data organizations that
quantum machine learning has never treated as a choice. We make that choice
explicit. \emph{Shot grouping} partitions a fixed measurement record into
disjoint groups and averages within each, tuning smoothly between the two
conventions with a single validated parameter and no additional quantum
executions. Across chaotic benchmarks, real laser dynamics, and sunspot records
under strict execution budgets, an intermediate grouping consistently
outperforms the standard expectation-value pipeline; matched controls show the
gain acts as correctly scaled regularization of the classical readout, and
unmitigated runs on two superconducting processors confirm the trend on every
task.
\end{abstract}
